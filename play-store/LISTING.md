## Full description (1234 / 4000)
Vegam Stories brings you short, beautifully illustrated stories that you can enjoy in three ways: read them as colourful comics, turn the pages of a book, or watch them as videos.

Read in Telugu, English or Hindi. Pick your language when you start and switch any time, even in the middle of a story.

★ THREE WAYS TO ENJOY EVERY STORY
• Comic: every page is a fully illustrated comic with speech bubbles
• Book: a calm, paper-like reader with real page turns
• Video: watch the story at the end, right inside the app

★ STORIES WORTH SHARING
• Moral stories and more
• New stories added regularly, with an optional alert when one arrives
• Share a story's moral with friends and family

★ READ YOUR WAY
• Choose text size, and a default, sepia or dark page
• Keep the screen on while you read
• Light and dark themes for the whole app
• Continue reading right where you left off

★ YOUR OWN LIBRARY
• Download stories and read them offline, with no internet needed
• Keep your favourites and bookmark any page
• Collect finished stories on your bookshelf
• Home screen widget for the latest story

★ SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
• No sign-up or login needed
• Free to read
